# My development portfolio

> This is a starter, not evidence of completed work. Replace these prompts with your own verified work before sharing.

## The outcome

What did you develop, who was it for, and where can someone see it? Add a link only if it is yours to share.

## My development stack

List the harnesses, models and versions, instructions, skills, integrations and other tools you actually used. Describe what you and each agent did. Do not list a tool you did not use.

## Decisions and delegation

What did you decide, change or delegate, and why? Record a meaningful change in approach and its result if you can verify it.

## Evidence and checks

What did you check, how did you check it, and what happened? Include inputs, dates, versions and links when they help someone repeat the checks and you have permission to share them. Separate your observations from an assistant's claims.

## Limitations and review

What failed, remains untested, or needs independent review? For safety-critical or regulated work, distinguish an exploratory artifact from a deliverable approved by a qualified professional.

## What I learned

What would you keep, adapt or try differently next time?

## Sources and permissions

Credit sources and collaborators, identify applicable licenses and record permissions. Do not publish secrets, personal data, customer work or other material without permission.
